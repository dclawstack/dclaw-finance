§ 01   Brand Architecture One company. One platform. One visual system.

A parent company, a flagship product.

One Convergence is the parent brand. DKube is its flagship AI/MLOps platform. The two marks operate on a clear hierarchy: One Convergence speaks to the enterprise relationship; DKube speaks to the practitioner.

Fig. 01 — Parent brand

The company.

Use the One Convergence wordmark when speaking on behalf of the company — corporate communications, partnerships, investor materials, and the enterprise homepage. It carries authority and continuity.

Apply when
  • Corporate site, careers, press releases
  • Customer agreements and contracts
  • Annual reports and investor decks
  • Multi-product or platform-wide announcements
Fig. 02 — Flagship product
DKubeTM

The platform.

Use the DKube mark inside the product, on the product site, and in technical materials addressed to ML engineers and platform teams. DKube is positioned as a confident, practitioner-first MLOps platform.

Apply when
  • Product UI, documentation, SDK
  • Developer marketing and DevRel
  • Pricing, technical white papers
  • Product-led launches and integrations

DKube turns the messy middle of machine learning — data, training, serving, governance — into a single, auditable pipeline.

Founded in 2014 and headquartered in Santa Clara, One Convergence builds platforms for organizations whose models matter. Our flagship product, DKube, is an end-to-end MLOps platform used by data science teams in financial services, life sciences, and government to take models from notebook to production without rebuilding their stack every quarter.

DKube runs on Kubernetes, integrates with the open-source tools teams already use — Kubeflow, MLflow, JupyterHub, Ray — and adds the parts that enterprise platforms typically lack: a unified data catalog, role-based access, model lineage, and policy-aware serving. It is a platform built for the people who maintain it, not just the people who demo it.

The brand reflects that posture. Editorial where it earns the right to be. Precise where it has to be. And — across product, marketing, and the long tail of enterprise paperwork — recognizably One Convergence.

# Define a DKube pipeline that fine-tunes and serves a model
from dkube import Pipeline, Step

pipe = Pipeline("resnet-finetune", workspace="acme-research")

@pipe.step(gpu=8, image="dkube/pytorch:2.3")
def train(dataset, epochs=40):
    model = load_pretrained("resnet-50")
    return model.fit(dataset, epochs=epochs)

@pipe.serve(replicas=2, autoscale=True)
def predict(model, image):
    return model.infer(image)

pipe.run()  # lineage, metrics, and lineage logged automatically
§ 07   Voice & Tone Plainspoken · technically credible · enterprise-aware

We respect the reader's time, and their intelligence.

We speak the language of practitioners — data scientists, ML engineers, platform leads, CIOs. We are technically credible by default and avoid the rehearsed vocabulary of AI marketing.

We say We don't say
DKube cuts training-job setup from days to minutes.
Specific · measurable
DKube supercharges your AI workflows and unleashes 10× productivity.
Hype · unverifiable
Run distributed training on Kubernetes without writing Helm charts.
Concrete · what it does
Revolutionize the way your team thinks about AI infrastructure.
Cliché · empty
Model lineage is automatic. Auditors get one URL.
Plain · ends with a benefit
Our game-changing compliance suite empowers stakeholders end-to-end.
Stuffed · five filler words
Bring your own cluster, or run on ours. Both work.
Direct · respects the choice
A best-in-class hybrid cloud-native platform for the modern AI enterprise.
Adjective soup
DKube ships every two weeks. Release notes are short on purpose.
Confident · self-aware
DKube is constantly evolving with cutting-edge features delivered seamlessly.
Vague · no actual claim
Always
  • → Lead with the verb
  • → Use real numbers when you have them
  • → Address the reader as you
  • → Sentence case in UI, headlines, buttons
  • → One idea per sentence
Never
  • × Revolutionize · supercharge · unleash · game-changing
  • × Synergy · leverage (as a verb)
  • × All-caps for emphasis in prose
  • × Three adjectives in a row
  • × Emoji in product copy
