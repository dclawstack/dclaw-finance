# REEL PROMPT — DClaw Finance

**Format:** 45–60 sec vertical reel (9:16) · Instagram / LinkedIn

---

## Visual Direction

Dark UI screen recording style. Purple `#7030A0` dominant with white cards on near-black background. Sharp 2px corners, no rounded blobs. Transitions: fast cuts (0.2s) timed to beat. No stock footage — only product screens and clean text cards.

Scene sequence:
1. **(0–4s)** Title card — white text on black: *"Your finance team just got an AI co-pilot."*
2. **(4–12s)** Dashboard screen — KPI cards animating up (₹2.4 Cr revenue, ₹18.6 L outstanding). 12-month revenue vs expenses line chart draws in.
3. **(12–20s)** Expense screen — user drags a receipt image. "Reading receipt…" spinner. Fields auto-fill. Category badge snaps to "Travel — AI suggested 94%".
4. **(20–28s)** Invoice detail — overdue badge visible. User clicks "Draft Reminder". AI email appears word-by-word in the dialog.
5. **(28–36s)** Budgets screen — red ⚠ card at 87% utilization. AI suggestion box fades in: *"Software spend is 87% of budget. Consider deferring 2 subscriptions."*
6. **(36–44s)** Chat screen — user types *"Which client brought the most revenue this quarter?"* AI responds with a ranked list in 1.5 seconds.
7. **(44–52s)** Reports screen — user hits "Generate Report". Executive summary streams in. KPI cards: ₹2.4 Cr revenue · ₹91 L expenses · ₹1.49 Cr profit.
8. **(52–60s)** End card — purple background, white logo: **DClaw Finance**. Tagline: *"Built for Indian enterprise. Powered by Claude."*

---

## Narration Script — Indian English, calm and confident, mid-pace

> "Finance teams in India spend hours chasing invoices, sorting receipts, and writing the same reminder emails — again and again.
>
> DClaw Finance changes that.
>
> Drop a receipt — it reads itself. Type an expense — it categorises itself. Invoice gone overdue? One click, AI writes the reminder.
>
> Your budgets warn you before they breach. Your clients are ranked by profitability. And when you need answers — just ask.
>
> Monthly reports that used to take half a day? Generated in seconds, in plain English, with full INR breakdowns.
>
> DClaw Finance. AI-native finance management — built for the way Indian businesses actually run."

---

## Audio Notes

- Voice: Indian English, RP-adjacent, male or female — calm authority, not sales-y. ElevenLabs voices: *Raj*, *Meera*, or *Arjun* work well.
- Background music: minimal lo-fi electronic, 90–100 BPM, drops slightly during narration pauses.
- No sound effects on UI interactions — keep it clean.
