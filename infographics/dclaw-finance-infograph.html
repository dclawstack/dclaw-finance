The platform

One platform.
All your finance data.

DClaw Finance is a full-stack enterprise financial management platform built on the One Convergence design system and powered by DKube's private AI infrastructure philosophy — your data, your infrastructure, full control.

It replaces fragmented spreadsheets, disconnected invoice tools, and reactive reporting with a single, AI-augmented system that knows your numbers as well as you do.

📊

Invoice & expense management

Full CRUD for invoices, line items, expenses, vendors. Category filters, status tracking, tax handling.

🤖

AI at every decision point

Auto-categorise, OCR receipts, draft reminders, detect anomalies, forecast cash flow, answer questions in plain English.

📈

Predictive financial intelligence

12-month trend charts, 3-month exponential smoothing forecast, client profitability scoring, budget guardrails.

🔒

Private, compliant, on-premise

Runs entirely on your infrastructure via Docker Compose or Kubernetes. No data leaves your environment.
